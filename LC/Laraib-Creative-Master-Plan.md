## 0. Executive Summary

Laraib Creative is a premium online stitching house for ladies' suits in Lahore. A customer books a stitching order online, sends her fabric by rider pickup, courier or drop-off at Garhi Shahu, picks a design, gives her measurements (saved to her profile), pays, and tracks the order through every stage until the stitched suit reaches her door. Every finished piece is photographed (with the customer's consent) for a "Stitched by Laraib" gallery and verified reviews. That gallery is the main marketing engine. Ready-to-wear (RTW) suits come in Phase 3, on the same catalog, cart and checkout.

**Market gap (from research, see §1A):** stitching in Lahore today is sold mostly through OLX and Facebook ads from neighbourhood tailors, priced at about Rs 650–1,400 for a simple suit. A few online stitching services with pickup already exist in Lahore (e.g. Sasta Darzi, Stitching World) and more in Karachi (Suikaar, Sohaay, SahiSe), so Laraib Creative will **not be the first** online tailoring service. But none of the ones checked offer a premium brand, online order tracking, saved measurement profiles, verified photo reviews or a delivery-date guarantee. The opportunity is to be **Lahore's first premium, trust-first tailoring brand**. Laraib Creative should not compete on price. It should sell **certainty**: on-time delivery dates, a fitting guarantee, saved measurements, live status updates and photo proof.

**Startup approach to payments:** no payment gateway at launch (it adds setup and per-transaction cost). Customers pay by bank transfer / JazzCash / Easypaisa / Raast to the account details shown on the site, then upload the payment slip on the website or send it on WhatsApp. Staff verify it in the admin panel. See §6.8.

1. **Laraib in-house rider pickup & delivery** (Lahore, primary option): our own uniformed delivery staff collect the fabric from the customer's door and return the stitched suit. See §6.12.
2. **Courier fabric to us** (outside Lahore): the site generates a printable/WhatsApp label addressed to the Garhi Shahu warehouse; the finished suit goes back by courier

**Why in-house riders are a big advantage:** customers hand over expensive fabric (often bridal or Eid fabric), and a known Laraib rider with ID, uniform and an order QR builds far more trust than an unknown courier. Riders can also take measurements at the door, collect a sample suit, collect COD, and bring back alterations. That is service no Lahore neighbourhood tailor offers.

## 5. UI / UX Design Direction — "Premium, not vibe-coded"

### 5.1 What makes sites look "Lovable/AI generated", and the rules against it
| Avoid | Do instead |
|---|---|
| Purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism everywhere | A restrained, editorial palette (plum/ivory/gold), mostly flat surfaces, texture from real fabric photography |
| Default shadcn cards in a 3-column grid with emoji icons | Editorial asymmetric layouts, large photography, generous whitespace, custom line-icon set (needle, thread, scissors, measuring tape) |
| Inter everywhere at one weight | A serif display + sans pairing with a proper type scale and tight tracking on headings |
| Generic "Get Started" hero with a blob | A real workshop video hero and a headline that names the promise and the delivery date |
| Animations on everything | Purposeful motion: about 3 signature moments, everything else subtle |
| Lorem-style marketing copy | Specific copy: "Fabric received → 7 days → at your door. Late? Stitching is free." |
| Rounded-2xl everything | A consistent radius system (4px / 12px), hairline borders, gold rules as dividers |

### 5.2 Design System (build in Figma first)
- Tokens: color, type scale, spacing (4/8 grid), radius, elevation, motion durations/easings
- Light theme first (luxury = ivory), dark "evening" theme optional
- Components: buttons, inputs, steppers, chips (design options), measurement input with illustration, timeline, gallery cards, review cards, price summary, toasts, bottom sheets (mobile)
- RTL-ready Urdu typography for future Urdu toggle
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, focus rings, 44px touch targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected

### 5.3 Signature Animations (Motion + GSAP + Lenis)
1. **Hero**: slow-motion fabric drape video with a masked text reveal of the headline
2. **"How it works" scroll story**: a pinned section where an SVG thread "stitches" a line between steps as the user scrolls (GSAP ScrollTrigger + SVG stroke-dashoffset)
3. **Before → After slider**: fabric to finished suit on the portfolio
4. **Order timeline**: a needle icon travels along the status line to the current stage
5. **Design customiser**: smooth crossfade of the neckline/sleeve illustration when options change; animated price counter
6. Micro-interactions: magnetic primary CTA, a subtle "thread" underline on link hover, shared-element transitions from gallery card to detail (View Transitions API), skeleton shimmer in brand colors, confetti made of tiny sequins on order confirmation (once, tasteful)
7. Smooth scroll (Lenis) on marketing pages only; never inside the dashboard

**Performance budget:** LCP < 2.0s on 4G mobile, CLS < 0.05, INP < 200ms. Animations use transform/opacity only, and heavy GSAP loads only on the pages that use it.

### 5.4 UX Principles
- **Mobile-first**: most Pakistani traffic comes from Instagram/TikTok on Android phones. Test on mid-range Android over 4G.
- **WhatsApp everywhere**: a floating WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message ("Hi, I want to ask about order #LC-1042")
- **Guest checkout + phone OTP login** (no password friction)
- **Trust signals** at every decision point: verified reviews count, "X suits stitched this month", fabric-safety guarantee, real workshop address
- **Measurement anxiety killer**: three options (standard size / send a sample suit / home measurement visit), plus a "Fit Guarantee" badge
- **Bilingual microcopy** with an English/Urdu toggle (Phase 2)

### 6.2 Smart Measurements — All Options

At the measurement step of the order wizard, the customer sees **six clear option cards**. She picks whichever she is comfortable with, and every option ends in a saved **Measurement Profile** she can reuse next time.

| # | Option | How it works | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **🏠 Home Measurement Visit** | Book a slot → a trained Laraib staff member visits and measures (female measurer by default) → measurements entered into the measurer's app on the spot → saved to her profile | Free in Zone A/B with an order, small fee in Zone C or without an order (adjusted against the first order) | First-time, bridal and formal customers; families who want several people measured |
| 2 | **👗 Send a Stitched Suit (Copy My Fit)** | She gives us a suit that already fits her well. Our rider collects it with the fabric (or she sends it by courier / drops it at Garhi Shahu) → the master tailor measures it → we stitch to match → the sample suit is returned with her order | Free | Customers who say "make it exactly like my favourite suit" (very common) |
| 3 | **📏 Laraib Standard Sizes (Brand-Benchmarked)** | Pick a size (XS–3XL) from the Laraib size chart, benchmarked against the sizing of Lahore's leading running brands (see below) | Free | Customers who already buy ready-made suits and know their size |
| 4 | **🏷️ "I Wear Size M in [Brand]" Matcher** | She picks the brand she usually buys from and her size there → the system maps it to the matching Laraib size + fit (regular/slim/loose) | Free | The fastest option: one tap |
| 5 | **✍️ Measure Yourself (Guided)** | Step-by-step guide with illustrations and a 10-second video per measurement, inch/cm toggle, live validation ("chest 12" looks wrong") | Free | Customers who know their measurements or have a measuring tape |
| 6 | **📱 Video-Call Measurement** | Book a 10-minute WhatsApp video call → our staff guide her through measuring herself → staff enter the measurements | Free | Customers outside Lahore and overseas |

Options can be **combined**: e.g. a standard size M + "make the sleeves 1 inch longer" + "trouser length 38 inches". Every option also has a **notes** box ("loose fit at the waist", "keep the shirt short").

**Laraib Standard Size Chart (how to build it)**
- Collect the published size guides of the leading Lahore/Pakistani women's ready-to-wear brands that customers already trust (for example Khaadi, Sapphire, Gul Ahmed / Ideas, Alkaram, J., Limelight, Generation, Maria B, Sana Safinaz). Also buy 2–3 pieces in common sizes from the top brands and measure them flat, because brands size differently and online charts are not always accurate.
- Work out a **Laraib chart** (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, 3XL) with chest, waist, hip, shoulder, armhole, sleeve length, shirt length, trouser length, trouser waist/hip and ankle (pancha) for each size
- Offer **fit preferences** per size: Slim / Regular / Comfort (loose)
- Separate charts per garment type (straight shirt, A-line/frock, kurti, maxi, trouser/shalwar/pants)
- Store each brand's sizes in an admin-editable **brand size mapping table**, so the matcher can be updated when brands change their sizing
- Keep it legally clean: show it as *"Size equivalent to brands you know"*, use brand names as text only (no logos), and add a note that Laraib Creative is not affiliated with these brands

**Measurement Profile features**
- Multiple profiles per account ("Me", "Ammi", "Sister", "Daughter")
- Each profile records **its source** (home visit / sample suit / standard size / brand match / self / video call) and who took it
- Version history + fitting feedback after each order ("tight at the chest", "perfect"), which the tailor sees on the next order
- After a home visit or sample-suit copy, the profile gets a **"Verified by Laraib"** badge
- **Fit Guarantee:** if a suit made from a verified profile or our standard size doesn't fit, alteration is free (rider collects and returns it)

**Admin & staff side**
- Measurement visit bookings show up in the **Dispatch & Riders** module as a job type (`measurement`), and can be combined with a fabric pickup in the same visit
- Measurer app (part of the rider PWA): body diagram with fields in the standard order, photo of the sample suit, customer signature
- Sample suits get their own **QR tag and custody tracking**, the same as fabric, so a customer's personal suit is never lost; the return is checked off at delivery
- The tailor's job card prints the final measurements + the source + fit notes

**Future (Phase 3):** AI body measurement from 2 photos (an API such as 3DLOOK or similar). Pilot it with verified customers first, comparing against their home-visit measurements.

### 6.8 Payments — Manual Payment + Slip Upload (startup mode, no gateway cost)

**Phase 1–2: no payment gateway integration.** It saves gateway setup and per-transaction fees and avoids weeks of merchant approval.

**What the customer sees on the payment step**
- Beautiful payment cards (with copy buttons) for each account:
  - **Bank account**: bank name, account title "Laraib Creative", account number, IBAN
  - **JazzCash** mobile account number + title
  - **Easypaisa** mobile account number + title
  - **Raast ID / QR code** (the customer scans it in her banking app)
- The amount to pay (full or advance, e.g. 50%), with the **order number as the payment reference** ("Write LC-1042 in the transfer note")
- Two ways to submit proof:
  1. **Upload slip / screenshot** on the site (image or PDF, phone camera supported) + optional transaction ID, sender name and amount
  2. **"Send slip on WhatsApp"** button → opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message: *"Assalam o Alaikum, payment slip for order LC-1042, amount Rs 4,500"*
- **Cash to rider**: the balance (or the full amount for trusted repeat customers) can be paid in cash to the Laraib rider at pickup or delivery
- Clear message: "Your order is reserved for 24 hours while we verify payment"

**Admin: Payment Verification module**
- A queue of slips waiting for verification (uploaded on the site or attached by staff from WhatsApp)
- View the slip next to the order amount, then **Approve / Reject (with reason) / Mark partial**
- On approval → order status becomes Confirmed + automatic WhatsApp confirmation and receipt
- On rejection → the customer is asked to re-upload
- Duplicate-slip check (the same transaction ID or image used twice is flagged)
- Unpaid orders auto-cancel or send a reminder after 24 hours
- Daily payment report per account (bank / JazzCash / Easypaisa / cash) for matching with bank statements
- Advance / balance tracking per order; balance due shown to the rider as "collect Rs X"

**Invoices & records:** PDF invoice/receipt after verification, refunds recorded manually (refund to the same account + proof upload), store credit, FBR-ready tax fields

**Later (Phase 3, when volume justifies the cost):** add a gateway (Safepay / PayFast / JazzCash / Easypaisa APIs) for instant confirmation. The database already stores `method` and `gateway` fields, so this is an add-on, not a rebuild. Keep manual transfer as an option even then, because many customers prefer it.

**WhatsApp setup for launch:** start with the free **WhatsApp Business app** (catalog, quick replies, labels like "Slip received", "Pickup today") on the business number. Move to the **WhatsApp Business API** (automatic order updates) in Phase 2 when order volume grows.

- **Zone C, Extended (15 km+):** DHA 7–9, Johar Town, Wapda Town, Bahria Town, Valencia, Lake City: higher fee, **fixed pickup days per area** (e.g. Bahria Town on Tue/Fri) so one rider covers that area in a single route
- Zones are drawn on a map in the admin, and the fee and slots are calculated automatically from the customer's pinned location
- Free pickup above a set order value or for Laraib Club members
- **Marketing focus follows the zones:** Instagram/Facebook ads geo-targeted to DHA, Gulberg, Cantt/Askari and Model Town first; flyers through society community WhatsApp/Facebook groups; tie-ups with salons and boutiques in those areas

**Customer experience**
- At checkout the customer picks **Pickup slot** (e.g. 11am–1pm, 3–5pm, 6–8pm) and **Delivery slot**
- Pin the exact location on a map + landmark field (important in Lahore addresses)
- WhatsApp: "Your rider Ali is on the way" with rider name, photo and call button; live location link (Phase 2)
- **Pickup OTP / QR handover:** the customer shows a 4-digit code and the rider scans it, so the fabric handover is recorded
- Rider photographs the fabric at the door → photo sent to the customer instantly (proof of what was collected)
- **Delivery OTP:** the suit is handed over only after the customer gives the code; proof-of-delivery photo + signature
- Optional services at the door: **home measurement** by a trained rider or female staff member, sample-suit collection, try-on wait (rider waits 10 minutes while the customer checks the fit; alteration pieces are taken straight back)
- Reschedule or cancel a slot from the tracking page

**Rider app (PWA in the same Next.js codebase, mobile-first, Urdu/English)**
- Login with phone OTP; shows only today's assigned jobs
- Job list sorted by route, with Google Maps navigation button and call/WhatsApp buttons
- Actions: Start route → Arrived → Scan order QR → Enter customer OTP → Take photos → Collect cash (COD/balance) → Complete
- Failed attempt reasons (customer not home, wrong address, refused) with photo + auto WhatsApp to the customer to reschedule
- Offline-tolerant (actions queue and sync when the signal returns)
- Cash wallet: running total of cash collected today
- Location shared only during an active shift

**Admin: Dispatch & Rider Management module**
- **Dispatch board:** today's pickups and deliveries on a map and a list, grouped by zone and slot; drag jobs to riders
- Auto-assign by zone + rider capacity; route optimisation (Google Routes API, or simply sorting by zone in Phase 1)
- Rider profiles: CNIC, photo, bike number, licence, emergency contact, zones, shift times, verification status
- Live rider locations (Phase 2), job status in real time
- **Cash reconciliation:** expected cash per rider vs. deposited at end of shift; shortage alerts; daily closing report
- Rider performance: jobs per day, on-time %, failed attempts, customer rating of the rider, fuel/commission payout
- Payout rules: fixed salary + per-job incentive + fuel allowance, calculated automatically each month
- Fabric chain of custody: Customer → Rider (scan) → Warehouse intake (scan) → Production → QC → Rider (scan) → Customer (OTP). Every handover is logged, so a missing fabric can always be traced.

**Operations & safety**
- Uniform, ID card and branded fabric bags (sealed, tamper-evident tag with the order QR)
- Rider background check (CNIC verification, references) before hiring
- Women-only pickup option (female staff) for customers who prefer it: a strong trust feature
- Peak planning: extra riders or part-timers in the 3 weeks before Eid and in wedding season
- Start with 2–3 riders on motorbikes; add a rider when daily jobs exceed about 20–25 per rider
- Courier APIs (PostEx/Leopards/TCS) remain only for orders outside Lahore and as overflow backup

### 7.3 Expenses, Accounts & Partnership Profit Sharing (50 / 50)

**Business structure**
| Partner | Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Partner A: Tech & Online Partner** (software engineer) | Online business | Website and admin system, online leads, social media and ads, customer support, WhatsApp, order intake, payment verification, reviews/gallery, analytics |
| **Partner B: Stitching Partner** (master tailor) | Production | Workshop at Garhi Shahu, cutting and stitching, karigars, quality, materials (threads, lining, buttons), measurement quality, delivery timelines |
| **Profit split** | **Net profit 50% / 50%** | Net profit = total revenue − all business expenses (calculated monthly) |

The admin dashboard makes the partnership **fully transparent**: both partners see the same live numbers, every expense has a receipt, and the monthly profit split is calculated automatically, so there is no argument about money.

**A. Income tracking (automatic)**
- Every verified payment (bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast, cash to rider, cash at the shop) is recorded automatically as income
- Walk-in and offline orders at the Garhi Shahu shop are entered in the admin too, so **all** income goes through one system
- Income by category: stitching, alterations, add-ons, delivery fees, urgency fees, (later) RTW sales
- Refunds and discounts are deducted automatically

**B. Expense management**
- **Add expense** form: date, category, amount, paid from (business bank / JazzCash / cash box / partner's own pocket), paid by (which partner or staff), vendor, notes, **receipt photo upload** (required above a set amount)
- **Expense categories** (admin-editable):

| Group | Categories |
|---|---|
| **Production** | Karigar/tailor wages (per piece or monthly), threads, lining, buttons, lace, interfacing, needles, machine oil, machine repair/maintenance, new machines & tools |
| **Delivery & riders** | Rider salaries, fuel allowance, per-job incentives, bike maintenance, courier charges (outside Lahore), fabric bags/packaging, QR tags |
| **Premises** | Shop/warehouse rent (Garhi Shahu), electricity (LESCO), gas, water, internet, generator/UPS fuel, cleaning, security |
| **Staff** | Support/office staff salaries, measurer (home visit staff) pay, staff meals, uniforms, bonuses (Eid) |
| **Marketing** | Meta/Instagram/TikTok ads, Google ads, influencer payments, photography/videography, printing (flyers, cards, tags), giveaways and discounts |
| **Technology** | Domain (laraibcreative.com), hosting (Vercel/DB), Cloudinary, email, WhatsApp API, SMS, software subscriptions, phones for riders |
| **Admin & legal** | Bank charges, wallet transaction fees, accountant, partnership deed/registration, taxes (FBR/PRA), licences |
| **Losses** | Damaged/lost fabric compensation, free re-stitching/rework, bad debts |
| **Other** | Miscellaneous (with a required note) |

- **Recurring expenses**: rent, salaries, subscriptions are added automatically each month (just confirm the amount)
- **Expense approval rule**: expenses above a set amount (e.g. Rs 10,000) need the **other partner's approval** in the dashboard before they count
- **Per-order cost tracking**: karigar wages and materials can be linked to an order → **profit per order** and per design category
- Monthly **budget per category** with alerts when spending exceeds it

**C. Capital, drawings & partner ledger**
- **Capital contributions**: record money each partner puts into the business (e.g. initial machines, rent deposit, laptop/software costs), so each partner's investment is known
- **Partner-paid expenses**: when a partner pays a business expense from their own pocket, it is credited to that partner's ledger and repaid from business funds
- **Drawings / advances**: money a partner takes during the month is recorded and adjusted against their profit share at month end
- **Partner ledger** per partner: opening balance + capital + share of profit + reimbursements − drawings = balance due/payable

**D. Monthly profit & loss + 50/50 settlement**
```
  Total revenue (all verified income)
− Refunds & discounts
= Net revenue
− Production costs (karigar wages, materials)
= Gross profit
− Operating expenses (rent, utilities, riders, staff, marketing, technology, admin, losses)
= Net profit (before tax)
− Business reserve (optional, e.g. 10% for machines, Eid stock, emergencies — agreed by both)
− Taxes (if applicable)
= Distributable profit
→ Partner A: 50%  |  Partner B: 50%
→ minus each partner's drawings/advances this month
→ = Final amount payable to each partner
```
- The **month-end closing** button locks the month (no more edits without both partners' approval), generates the P&L and the settlement statement as a PDF, and both partners tap **"Approve"**
- If a month makes a **loss**, it is shared 50/50 too (or carried forward to next month, as written in the partnership agreement); the system supports both rules
- Settlement history: every past month's P&L, split and payout is kept

**E. Cash & account management**
- Balances for each money account: business bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa, shop cash box, cash with riders
- Daily cash closing for the shop and riders; transfers between accounts (e.g. "cash deposited to bank")
- Bank reconciliation: mark each bank statement entry as matched to an income or expense

**F. Finance reports & dashboard**
- Revenue vs expenses vs net profit (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) with charts
- Expense breakdown by category (where the money goes)
- Profit per order, per garment type and per area/zone
- Cost per pickup/delivery, marketing cost per order (CAC), break-even point per month
- Partner dashboard: "This month so far: revenue Rs X, expenses Rs Y, estimated profit share Rs Z each"
- Export to Excel/PDF for the accountant and tax filing

**G. Audit & trust**
- Every finance entry records who added it, when, and every edit (audit log); entries are never deleted, only reversed
- Receipts stored privately; both partners get a WhatsApp/email summary every week and at month end

> **Recommendation:** before launch, sign a written **partnership deed** (on stamp paper, ideally drafted by a lawyer, and registered with the Registrar of Firms under the Partnership Act, 1932) covering: the 50/50 split, each partner's duties, capital contributions, how losses are shared, the drawing limits, who owns the brand "Laraib Creative", the domain and the software/code, what happens if a partner leaves, and how disputes are resolved. Also ask an accountant about FBR and Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA) tax registration. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

## 10. Integrations & Automation Flows
- **Order placed** → status Pending Payment → customer uploads slip (or sends it on WhatsApp) → Payment Under Verification → staff approve → Confirmed → WhatsApp confirmation + receipt + pickup slot details
- **No slip after 6h / 24h** → reminder → auto-cancel and release the capacity slot
- **Fabric received** (QR scan) → photo sent to customer → production countdown starts
- **Status change** → WhatsApp template message
- **Pickup booked** → rider job created in the slot → morning-of WhatsApp "Rider arriving 11am–1pm" → rider scan + OTP + fabric photo → status Fabric in Transit → warehouse scan → Fabric Received
- **Ready** → QC photo to customer → Lahore: delivery rider job auto-created for the chosen slot / outside Lahore: courier auto-booked → tracking link sent
- **Delivered** (rider OTP confirmation or courier webhook) → after 2 days a review request (including "Rate your rider") → after 30 days a re-order nudge
- **Rider shift end** → cash closing reminder → mismatch alert to the manager
- **Abandoned wizard** → reminder after 1h and 24h
- **Late risk** → admin alert when an order is behind schedule relative to its promised date
